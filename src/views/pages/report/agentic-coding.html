AI Drives 14x Surge in New Bitcoin Integrations

AI has transformed how software gets built, with bitcoin at the center: Breez reports that new bitcoin integrations have surged 14x in the agentic coding era, with record demand for everything from crypto wallets, exchanges and swap services to messengers, games, and social apps.

Demand for adding bitcoin to apps and services has never been higher. Crypto wallets, exchanges, swaps, and bridging services are not only integrating faster than ever before, but a whole new generation of apps and services is bringing bitcoin into products where it has never operated before: an encrypted messenger where bitcoin moves inside the conversation. An app that identifies plants and wildlife. A travel eSIM service. A wellness app. A cooking recipe app. And many more.

New data from Breez, whose free and open-source SDK powers instant, non-custodial bitcoin and stablecoins in apps and services around the world, shows how quickly demand for instant bitcoin is growing, and exactly when it took off.

The shift began in early 2025, when Anthropic released Claude Code. Agents stopped merely suggesting software and started building it, and the cost of shipping collapsed: projects scoped in quarters became projects scoped in days. By September, when Claude Code gained major new autonomy capabilities, the change had already brought a wave of inbound demand to Breez, and it has kept growing ever since.

Breez has seen new bitcoin integrations surge 14x in the era of AI coding, and now sees as many new teams starting an integration in days as it once did in a month. Crypto wallets, exchanges, and swap services that know bitcoin best are integrating faster than they ever have.

Alongside them, an entirely new market of products has developed, and it's growing rapidly: with everything from messengers to gaming and wellness to travel apps, all adding bitcoin. This year, requests from non-crypto apps are up 5x versus last year, and they're accelerating: the second quarter came in 61% above the first.

As technology moves faster, Breez is seeing more builders look for ways to move value just as freely: to anyone, anywhere, instantly, without accounts, approvals, or geographic restrictions. That's why mainstream teams are turning to bitcoin to bring permissionless money into their products.

"AI didn't make building on bitcoin easy. We did that already. What's really happening is AI accelerating everything, and software moving that fast needs money that can keep up: value that moves instantly, anywhere, without anyone's permission. That's why builders are choosing bitcoin."

Roy Sheinfeld, CEO of Breez

Speed is what smashed open the door for builders, and the best proof comes from the companies that know how hard it used to be to build with bitcoin. The exchange platform Exolix went from requesting an API key to production in under two weeks. Cake Wallet, the popular non-custodial multi-currency wallet with nearly two million users, spent years fighting bitcoin's complexities; but this year that all changed, when it built a working integration over a single weekend with one engineer.

"It was fast. No hassle. Easy to use, easy to set up. And that's what the Breez SDK brought to the world."

Vikrant Sharma, CEO of Cake Wallet and Radar

Breez is already seeing the next step of the AI building revolution, with some inbound requests not written by people at all: developers are now simply requesting their AI agents handle the implementation. Agents can't open bank accounts or wait for settlement windows; they need ways to move value easily, globally, and autonomously. The tools that build the software have started choosing what it runs on, and they are choosing bitcoin.

Notes for reader

Why This Matters

It's a measurable change in what is being built right now. Breez sits at the stage where an app or service decides how it will move money, so its inbound requests (teams that request an API key to build with the Breez SDK) show what builders are choosing before the products are released. The requests now come from apps that have nothing to do with crypto, and they're arriving at a rate Breez had never seen before the rise of agentic coding.

Why Now

AI transformed the cost of building. Since coding agents, such as Claude Code, arrived in early 2025, teams can now build and ship an app in days rather than months, and anyone who can describe what they want can now build it too. More products are being made, by more people, faster, and each of them is making a decision on how value moves through their product.

Why Bitcoin

Two reasons. First, builders need money that matches the increased pace of development: an app can now be built and released in days, and it cannot wait weeks for a bank, payment processor, or card network's approval. Second, they want their users to move value to anyone, anywhere, in the moment, without opening accounts, asking for approval or being limited by geography. Therefore, money has to flow without a gatekeeper. Bitcoin is the permissionless money that does both, which is why builders are choosing it in record numbers.

What's the Methodology

Figures are drawn from Breez's analysis of inbound integration requests received between May 2023 and August 2026. The "pre-AI" baseline is calendar years 2023 and 2024. Requester categories are classified from the text of each request; the classification is directional. Further details are available to reporters on request.
