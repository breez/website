Moving Your Funds Out of Misty Breez

The service Misty Breez relies on is no longer available. As a result, on-chain and Lightning payments cannot be made in the app.

To mitigate this, we added the ability to send and receive with a Liquid address.

How to Move Your Funds

You can move your funds to any wallet that can receive an on-chain bitcoin transaction.

We recommend Glow, a bitcoin app built for everyone, powered by the Breez SDK.

You can learn more about Glow here: breez.technology/glow

Since the built-in service in Misty Breez is no longer available, you can use an external service to transfer from Liquid bitcoin (L-BTC) to on-chain bitcoin. Some examples are:

  • SideShift.ai
  • Changelly
  • FlypMe

These are external services, operated by third parties and not by Breez. Breez does not run them, does not endorse them, and cannot help with them. Check their fees, limits and terms yourself, and use them at your own discretion.
